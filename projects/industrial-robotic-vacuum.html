A useful machine for school

I designed and built a robotic vacuum for my school. The build combined the physical cleaning mechanism with a mobile platform.

Making the airflow components

The project included a printed cyclone vacuum assembly. Experimenting with its rotating components later informed the impeller work for my go-kart project.

Documented in motion

The project was recorded as completed in May 2022, with videos of the machine operating. The linked demonstrations show the build in use.
