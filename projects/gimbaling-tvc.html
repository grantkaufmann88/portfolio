The mechanical question

I explored a 3D-printed gimbal for pitch-and-yaw control as part of an early rocket project. The project brought together mechanism design and experimental ground testing.

A test that exposed a material limit

Early ground-test hardware failed to retain its geometry under heat. The result underlined the gap between a part that works in CAD and one that survives its operating environment. The record does not establish a successful guided flight.
