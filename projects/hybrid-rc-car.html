Combining two power sources

I designed a conversion for a Traxxas nitro RC car to add electric power and an automatic starting system. The project is recorded as completed in May 2023.

A connection to larger projects

The interest in integrating combustion engines and electric machines also appears in my go-kart’s starter-generator system. Both projects explore the interfaces between motors, engines, and power transmission.
