Opening the workshop to more people

As captain of mechanical design, I helped teach and mentor a group of 25 students, many new to engineering, toward building a competitive robot for spring 2024.

Beyond the robotics team

I also worked as a teaching assistant with Mr. Martinez at Cambridge Rindge and Latin. I directly taught a group of six students through project-based Arduino work, helped develop curriculum, participated in faculty interviews, and introduced younger students to technical programs.

Continuing to give back

My later application materials describe returning to volunteer and mentor with the robotics team. Helping someone else understand a mechanism or get their first project working remains a meaningful part of engineering for me.
